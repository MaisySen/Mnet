## Narrow export route to assess

15CFR740.17(a)(1)(i) authorizes qualifying exports/reexports/transfers for internal development or production of new products by private-sector end users headquartered in a Supplement3 country, without classification requests or the specified classification/sales reports. Its note includes individuals not acting for a foreign government and private commercial firms. Official Supplement3 lists Australia.

The resolved one-owner test makes this route a better fit to assess than a general public or third-party beta. Preserve its internal-development restriction. Do not silently treat an eventual production release, unrelated tester, corporate deployment or Multies rollout as covered. Section740.17 has scope and other restrictions beyond its short summary; the applicable regulations and actual parties/end use control the decision.

Before accepting the Play export certification, verify the final distribution is still limited to that owner, identify all actual parties/end use, check relevant restrictions and keep the final binary/source/dependency inventory. The console's certification covers compliance and authorization for export, not merely awareness that encryption exists. No exporter filing, importer undertaking or whole-product classification has been made here.

## Hosting facts verified for privacy preparation

A current read of the configured service confirms Render free compute, Singapore region, one Docker instance and no persistent disk. The deployed command stores control state under a temporary local path. Its service environment currently exposes only the admin-password variable; no identity-provider settings appear there. The deployed source revision remains separate from the latest Android source. Do not infer that every source feature is enabled live.

Render documents loss of local filesystem changes on redeploy, restart or idle spin-down. That is an availability/data-loss limit, not a complete deletion process. Provider runtime logs have their own retention based on the workspace plan; a free compute instance does not establish the workspace plan or a precise provider-log retention duration. Neither provider-side backups/log streams nor support-mailbox handling have been fully verified. No service, plan, deployment, credential or logging setting was changed for this check.

The [privacy notice review draft](android-privacy-notice-draft.md) still needs those actual operator details and an adopted deletion process. Do not call it a published policy or put its source-file URL into the Play privacy-policy field.
